Frequently Asked Questions

Is Glow Hop free?

Yes! Glow Hop is completely free to play, with no advertisements, subscriptions, or in-app purchases in the current release.

How do I unlock new levels?

Complete each level to unlock the next. Glow Hop features 30 story levels across three worlds: Starry Night, Beyond the Stars, and Light the Sky.

How do I unlock Arcade modes?

Space Chaos unlocks after completing Level 20.

THE 300 unlocks after completing Level 30.

Does Glow Hop require an internet connection?

No. Normal gameplay can be enjoyed offline.

How do I save my progress?

Glow Hop automatically saves your progress locally on your device.

How do I report a bug?

Email the developer using the support address above. Include as much detail as possible so the issue can be investigated.
