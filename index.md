Glow Hop — Privacy Policy

Effective Date: November 13, 2026
Developer: Joshua Forbey

Overview

Glow Hop is a single-player mobile arcade game developed by Joshua Forbey.

We respect your privacy and aim to provide a gaming experience without unnecessary data collection.

Information We Collect

Glow Hop does not require users to create an account or provide personal information.

The current release does not include advertisements, active in-app purchases, or developer-operated analytics services.

Subject to verification of the final released build and its included third-party software, Glow Hop does not collect or transmit personal information to the developer.

Game Progress and Local Storage

Glow Hop may save game progress, unlocked levels, settings, and high scores locally on your device.

This information is used to maintain your progress and is not transmitted to developer-operated servers.

You can remove locally stored game information by deleting the app, subject to any backups maintained by your device or operating system.

Third-Party Services

Glow Hop is distributed through Apple's App Store. Apple may process information associated with downloads, purchases, diagnostics, or device services under Apple's own privacy practices.

Glow Hop does not intentionally integrate third-party advertising or tracking services.

Children's Privacy

Glow Hop is designed for a general audience and does not knowingly request or collect personal information from children.

Data Sharing

Glow Hop does not sell personal information or intentionally share player information with advertisers or data brokers.

Changes to This Policy

This policy may be updated if Glow Hop introduces new features or changes its data practices. Any updates will be posted on this page with a revised effective date.
